# Appointment Rate

Appointment Rate measures how effectively contacted or qualified leads progress to a scheduled sales interaction.

## Two recommended versions

### Contacted-basis

`scheduled_appointments / contacted_leads`

### Qualified-basis

`scheduled_appointments / qualified_leads`

Always label the denominator.

## Appointment types

Keep appointment type as a separate field when possible:

- office meeting
- site visit
- phone call
- video call / online presentation
- broker meeting

## Common errors

- counting cancelled appointments as completed
- combining scheduled and completed appointments
- comparing teams using different denominators
- failing to deduplicate rescheduled meetings

## Companion metric

Use Show-up Rate to distinguish appointment creation from appointment quality and execution.
