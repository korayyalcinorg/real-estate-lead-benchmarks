# Qualification Rate

Qualification Rate measures how many successfully contacted leads meet the documented criteria for active sales follow-up.

## Recommended default formula

`qualified_leads / contacted_leads`

## Typical qualification dimensions

- budget fit
- purchase timeline
- preferred project or location
- property type
- financing / payment readiness
- investment or lifestyle intent
- willingness to continue the sales conversation

## Important

Qualification criteria must be written down before benchmark comparisons are made. Otherwise different teams may label leads differently.

## Alternative view

You may also calculate `qualified_leads / total_valid_leads`, but label it clearly as lead-to-qualified conversion.

## Segment by

- source
- campaign
- project
- market
- language
- sales rep
- response-time band
