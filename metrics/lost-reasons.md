# Lost Reason Analysis

Lost reasons explain why opportunities fail to progress. They should be standardized rather than stored only as free text.

## Recommended categories

- no_response
- budget
- timing
- location
- project_mismatch
- financing
- competitor
- duplicate
- invalid
- not_interested
- other

## Reporting

For each lost reason, report:

- count
- share of lost leads
- share of total valid leads
- source/campaign distribution
- project distribution
- response-time distribution

## Why it matters

A high volume of `no_response` can indicate a follow-up or speed-to-lead issue. A high volume of `budget` or `project_mismatch` may indicate targeting, positioning or qualification issues.

## Data quality rule

Avoid generic values such as `lost`, `bad lead` or `not suitable` when a more useful standardized reason can be recorded.

## Free-text notes

Free text can support internal analysis, but should not be published in public benchmark datasets because it may contain personally identifiable information.
