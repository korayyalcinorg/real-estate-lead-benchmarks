# Conversion Rate

Conversion Rate should always specify the start and end stages being compared.

## Lead-to-Sale Conversion

`won_leads / total_valid_leads`

This is useful for understanding the commercial output of the entire acquisition funnel.

## Contact-to-Sale Conversion

`won_leads / contacted_leads`

This helps separate lead quality and sales execution from reachability.

## Qualified-to-Sale Conversion

`won_leads / qualified_leads`

This helps evaluate qualification quality and sales effectiveness after qualification.

## Appointment-to-Sale Conversion

`won_leads / completed_appointments`

This helps evaluate late-funnel effectiveness.

## Segment by

- source
- campaign
- project
- market
- sales representative
- response-time band
- lead score
- appointment type

## Important

Do not compare conversion rates across teams unless stage definitions and attribution windows are compatible.
