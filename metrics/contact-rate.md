# Contact Rate

Contact Rate measures the percentage of valid leads that resulted in successful two-way communication.

## Default formula

`contacted_leads / total_valid_leads`

## What counts as contacted?

Recommended definition: a two-way interaction occurred. A dial attempt, delivered message or sent e-mail by itself should not be counted as successful contact.

## Useful segmentations

Compare Contact Rate by:

- source
- campaign
- project
- market
- language
- sales representative
- response-time band
- lead score

## Common measurement errors

- counting attempted leads as contacted
- leaving duplicates in the denominator
- mixing invalid/test leads into total leads
- using inconsistent definitions across teams

## Companion metrics

Contact Rate should be read together with Speed-to-Lead, attempt count, qualification rate and conversion rate.
