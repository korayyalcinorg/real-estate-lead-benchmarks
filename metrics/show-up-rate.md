# Show-up Rate

Show-up Rate measures the percentage of scheduled appointments that actually take place.

## Formula

`completed_appointments / scheduled_appointments`

## Recommended statuses

Use distinct appointment statuses:

- scheduled
- completed
- cancelled_by_lead
- cancelled_by_team
- rescheduled
- no_show

## Why this matters

A team can report a high Appointment Rate while still producing weak commercial outcomes if many appointments are cancelled or missed.

## Useful segmentations

Compare Show-up Rate by:

- appointment type
- lead source
- campaign
- project
- sales representative
- days between booking and appointment
- reminder workflow

## Common error

Do not treat a rescheduled appointment as both a failed appointment and a new independent opportunity without documenting the counting rule.
